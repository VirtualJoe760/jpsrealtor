# The Complete Guide to Public Golf in the Coachella Valley

## More than 40 courses you can play without a membership

The Coachella Valley has more than 100 golf courses, and a surprising number of them welcome anyone with a tee time. From a lighted par-3 in Indio to the island green at PGA West, this guide covers every public, resort and daily-fee course from Indio to Desert Hot Springs, with what golfers love about each one and the neighborhoods closest to the first tee.

It's organized east to west, city by city, starting in Indio and ending in Desert Hot Springs. Each course lists its official website (the best place for more photos, rates and tee times) and links to nearby neighborhoods on our site, where you can browse homes and community details.

**A few tips before you book:**

- **Overseed season:** Most desert courses close for a few weeks between mid-September and late November to plant winter grass. By Thanksgiving nearly everything is open and at its greenest.

- **Peak vs. summer:** January through April brings the best weather and the busiest tee sheets. Summer rounds are much less expensive if you play early.

- **City-owned courses:** Desert Willow (Palm Desert), Indian Wells Golf Resort, SilverRock (La Quinta), Tahquitz Creek (Palm Springs) and The Lights at Indio are owned by their cities, and several offer resident rates or golf cards. Ask the pro shop.

- **Semi-private clubs:** Several country clubs sell public tee times when members aren't using them. Availability changes by season, so call ahead.

## Indio

### The Lights at Indio Golf Course (Indio Municipal)

![Aerial night view of The Lights at Indio Golf Course](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/21718.jpg)

*Photo: The Lights at Indio Golf Course via GolfNow*

**18 holes, par 54 · City-owned, public** · [indiogolf.com](https://www.indiogolf.com/)

Indio's municipal course has been around since 1965 and is the only course in the Coachella Valley lit for night golf. It's also one of the longest par-3 courses in the country, with holes from roughly 110 to 240 yards, so you'll hit nearly every club in the bag. Golfers say playing under the lights is a blast, the staff is friendly and it's perfect for beginners, families and anyone dialing in their irons.

**Nearby neighborhoods:** [Terra Lago](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/indio/terra-lago), about a mile east (see below), and [Heritage Palms](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/indio/heritage-palms-country-club).

### Heritage Palms Golf Club

![View from the 17th tee at Heritage Palms Golf Club](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/21714.jpg)

*Photo: Heritage Palms Golf Club via GolfNow*

**18 holes, par 72 · Public** · [heritagepalmsindio.com](https://www.heritagepalmsindio.com/)

An Arthur Hills design from 1996 with the mountains close enough to touch. It's one of the highest-rated courses in the valley on GolfPass, where golfers praise its fast, true greens and manicured fairways, and nearly everyone who reviews it recommends it. GolfPass also calls it one of the east valley's best value rounds.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Heritage Palms Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/indio/heritage-palms-country-club): a guard-gated 55+ community of single-level homes built around the course from the late 1990s to early 2000s.

### Big Rock Golf Course at Indian Springs

![The signature par-3 5th hole at Big Rock with the Santa Rosa Mountains](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/14171.jpg)

*Photo: Big Rock Golf Course at Indian Springs via GolfNow*

**18 holes, par 72 · Public** · [bigrockgolf.com](https://www.bigrockgolf.com/)

A John Gurley design dating to 1962 and completely rebuilt around 2000. Big Rock is all about fun: forgiving fairways, quick, true greens, a friendly staff and the rock-and-roll-themed Big Rock Pub afterward. Reviewers repeatedly call it a great value, and one visiting golfer on a two-week Southern California trip said it had the best conditions of any course they played.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Indian Springs](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/indio/indian-springs): about 720 single-family homes built around the course starting in 2002.

### Indian Palms Country Club & Resort

![A hole over the water at Indian Palms Country Club](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/47177.jpg)

*Photo: Indian Palms Country Club & Resort via GolfNow*

**27 holes (Mountain, Royal & Indian nines) · Public** · [indianpalms.com](https://www.indianpalms.com/)

A course with real history: it began on land tied to aviator Jackie Cochran's ranch. Today it offers three distinct nines, hundreds of mature trees (rare for desert golf) and water on 15 of the 27 holes. The Royal nine is the most open and friendly to the average player, while the Mountain nine rewards accuracy. Its director of golf sums it up as a country-club experience for the budget-conscious golfer, and there's a boutique hotel steps from the first tee.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Indian Palms Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/indio/indian-palms-country-club): a mix of single-family homes and condos on the course, with a wide range of ages and sizes.

### The Golf Club at Terra Lago: North & South

![Elevated view from the 3rd hole on the North Course at Terra Lago](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/95406.jpg)

*Photo: The Golf Club at Terra Lago via GolfNow*

**36 holes (two 18s), par 72 · Public** · [golfterralago.com](https://golfterralago.com/)

Formerly Landmark Golf Club, Terra Lago hosted the PGA Tour's Skins Game from 1999 to 2002, and the clubhouse walls still show off those years. Both courses are Schmidt-Curley designs with dramatic elevation changes. The North features an island-green par 3 and a 7th tee that many call one of the best views in the valley; the South has the "Boxcar" hole, where you cross the creek on an old railcar bridge. GolfPass calls the North one of the most underrated plays in the region.

**Nearby neighborhoods:** [Terra Lago](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/indio/terra-lago), with single-family homes from 2005 to the present around a 20-acre lake, and [Four Seasons at Terra Lago](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/indio/four-seasons-at-terra-lago), a guard-gated 55+ community next door.

### Shadow Hills Golf Club: South & North

![A green with water and bunkers at Shadow Hills Golf Club](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/95843.jpg)

*Photo: Shadow Hills Golf Club via GolfNow*

**South: 18 holes, par 71 · North: 18-hole par 54 · Public** · [shadowhillsgolfclub.com](https://www.shadowhillsgolfclub.com/)

Two courses inside Sun City Shadow Hills, both open to the public. The South is a full-length championship layout, and the North is a beautifully designed par-3 course with water, bunkers and holes from under 100 yards to about 200. The North was ranked among the top public short courses in the country for 2026, and reviewers love it for short-game practice.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Sun City Shadow Hills](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/indio/sun-city-shadow-hills): a guard-gated 55+ community of about 3,400 homes.

### Eagle Falls Golf Course at Fantasy Springs

![Eagle Falls Golf Course at Fantasy Springs Resort Casino](https://www.fantasyspringsresort.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/Course-Description1.jpg)

*Photo: Fantasy Springs Resort Casino*

**18 holes, par 72 · Public** · [fantasyspringsresort.com/golf](https://www.fantasyspringsresort.com/golf/)

A Clive Clark design from 2007 at Fantasy Springs Resort Casino, owned by the Cabazon Band of Mission Indians. Golfweek named it a top new course when it opened and has ranked it among the top casino courses in the country. The par-3 6th is a nod to Royal Troon's famous "Postage Stamp," and golfers consistently rave about its conditions, from the range to the bunkers to the greens.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Terra Lago](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/indio/terra-lago), just minutes away.

## La Quinta

### PGA West: Stadium, Nicklaus Tournament & Greg Norman

![The Pete Dye Stadium Course at PGA West](https://www.pgawest.com/Images/Library/hp-pete-dye-stadium-course.jpg)

*Photo: PGA WEST*

**Three 18-hole resort courses, par 72 · Open to the public** · [pgawest.com](https://www.pgawest.com/)

The Pete Dye Stadium Course is one of the most famous and demanding public courses in the country, best known for the island-green 17th, nicknamed "Alcatraz." It's part of the PGA Tour's American Express rotation along with the Jack Nicklaus Tournament Course. The Greg Norman Course offers a desert-style test with wide fairways and native areas. Golfers who make the trip consistently call the Stadium Course a bucket-list round.

**Nearby neighborhoods:** [PGA West Stadium](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/la-quinta/pga-stadium), [PGA West Greg Norman](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/la-quinta/pga-greg-norman) and [PGA West Nicklaus Tournament](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/la-quinta/pga-nicklaus-tournament): condos, villas and single-family homes along each course.

### PGA West Pete Dye Mountain & Dunes Courses (La Quinta Resort)

![The Pete Dye Mountain Course against the Santa Rosa Mountains](https://www.pgawest.com/Images/Library/hp-pete-dye-mountain-course.jpg)

*Photo: PGA WEST*

**Two 18-hole resort courses, par 72 · Open to the public** · [pgawest.com](https://www.pgawest.com/)

The original Pete Dye courses at La Quinta Resort & Club, now played under the PGA West banner. The Mountain Course hugs the Santa Rosa Mountains with dramatic rock-framed greens, and the Dunes Course is a strategic, links-inspired layout. Both have been through recent greens restoration and are prized for their scenery.

**Nearby neighborhoods:** [Legacy Villas](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/la-quinta/legacy-villas), with villas and townhomes built 2005–2007 at the resort, and [Laguna de la Paz](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/la-quinta/laguna-de-la-paz), a quiet neighborhood of single-family homes from around 1989.

### SilverRock Resort: Arnold Palmer Classic Course

![View from the 16th tee surrounded by mountains at SilverRock Resort](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/14655.jpg)

*Photo: SilverRock Resort via GolfNow*

**18 holes, par 72 · City-owned, public** · [silverrock.org](https://www.silverrock.org/)

La Quinta's first municipal course, an Arnold Palmer design from 2005 that hosted the Bob Hope Classic. At more than 7,200 yards from the tips, it's one of the longest courses in the desert, set right at the base of the Santa Rosa Mountains. Reviewers praise its wide-open layout, which is forgiving for golfers who spray a few, along with a good pace of play and friendly staff.

**Nearby neighborhoods:** [The Citrus](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/la-quinta/the-citrus), with single-family homes built around 2000, and [La Quinta Fairways](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/la-quinta/la-quinta-fairways), with late-1990s single-family homes, both close to the course.

### Trilogy Golf Club at La Quinta

![Lake and mountain views at Trilogy Golf Club at La Quinta](https://www.mytrilogylife.com/laquinta/wp-content/uploads/sites/33/2024/12/TLQ-Golf-Photo-Gallery-1.jpg)

*Photo: Trilogy Golf Club at La Quinta*

**18 holes, par 72 · Public** · [mytrilogylife.com/laquinta/golf](https://www.mytrilogylife.com/laquinta/golf/)

A Gary Panks design that hosted the PGA Tour's Skins Game, open to the public inside the Trilogy community. Golfers rave about the staff, the value (range balls are included with a round) and the views of the Santa Rosa and Coral mountains.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Trilogy at La Quinta](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/la-quinta/trilogy): a guard-gated, age-qualified golf community of about 1,240 homes.

## Indian Wells

### Indian Wells Golf Resort: Celebrity & Players

![Aerial view of Indian Wells Golf Resort](https://www.indianwellsgolfresort.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/8991/2023/07/home5.jpg?w=1024)

*Photo: Indian Wells Golf Resort*

**36 holes (two 18s), par 72 · City-owned, public** · [indianwellsgolfresort.com](https://www.indianwellsgolfresort.com/)

Owned by the City of Indian Wells and managed by Troon, this is one of the most popular public plays in Southern California. The Celebrity Course, designed by Clive Clark, is known for its waterfalls, color and mountain backdrops. The Players Course reopened in December 2025 after a $13 million renovation led by its original architect, John Fought, and in fall 2026 it hosted the Epson Tour Championship, the LPGA's season-ending qualifying event. The clubhouse is also home to Kestrel, a Richard Blais restaurant. The resort has ranked among Condé Nast Traveler's top California golf resorts and hosted Golf Channel's *Big Break*.

**Nearby neighborhoods:** [Indian Wells Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/indian-wells/indian-wells-country-club), with single-family homes and condos from the 1970s–90s next door, and [Desert Horizons Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/indian-wells/desert-horizons-country-club), mostly late-1980s single-family homes just north.

## Palm Desert

### Desert Willow Golf Resort: Firecliff & Mountain View

![The 16th hole on the Firecliff Course at Desert Willow](https://www.desertwillow.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/9291/2025/06/DWFirecliff16_0084.jpg?w=1024)

*Photo: Desert Willow Golf Resort*

**36 holes (two 18s), par 72 · City-owned, public** · [desertwillow.com](https://www.desertwillow.com/)

Owned by the City of Palm Desert and widely considered the benchmark for public golf in the desert. Both courses use native desert landscaping instead of wall-to-wall turf, and both are beautiful to look at and genuinely challenging. Firecliff is the more dramatic of the two, and its greens were recently converted to MiniVerde Bermuda. Golfers often describe Desert Willow as the closest thing to a private-club experience that's open to everyone.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Desert Willow](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-desert/desert-willow): newer villas and condominiums, mostly built 2018–2019, inside the resort.

### JW Marriott Desert Springs: Palm & Valley Courses

![The Palm Course at JW Marriott Desert Springs](https://mds-assets.marriott.com/cdn-cgi/image/f=auto/cms-platform-for-marriott/ctdca-desert-springs-golf-club/site-images/migrated-images/palm-9.jpg?cropW=1800\&cropH=808\&width=1800\&height=808)

*Photo: Desert Springs Golf Club, JW Marriott*

**36 holes (two 18s), par 72 · Resort, open to the public** · [golfdesertsprings.com](https://www.golfdesertsprings.com/)

Two courses lined with lakes and palms at one of the valley's signature resorts, known for colorful landscaping and resort-level service.

**Nearby neighborhoods:** [Monterey Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-desert/monterey-country-club) and [Palm Valley Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-desert/palm-valley-country-club), both established, gated golf communities of condos and villas a short drive along Country Club Drive.

### Classic Club

![The 15th green at Classic Club with snow-capped mountains](https://www.classicclubgolf.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/8937/2023/10/15-green-to-16-green-snow-mountains.png)

*Photo: Classic Club*

**18 holes, par 72 · Daily fee** · [classicclubgolf.com](https://www.classicclubgolf.com/)

An Arnold Palmer design that opened in 2006 and hosted the PGA Tour's Bob Hope Classic from 2006 to 2008. It plays to more than 7,300 yards, with over 30 acres of water and rolling, lush terrain that feels unlike typical desert golf. Troon manages it, and it regularly appears on lists of the best courses you can play in California; one Golf Collective ranking put it at the top of all public courses in the valley.

**Nearby neighborhoods:** [Spanish Walk](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-desert/spanish-walk), with condos and townhomes built 2008–2015 just south of the course, and [Desert Falls Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-desert/desert-falls-country-club).

### Marriott's Shadow Ridge Golf Club

![The 18th green at Shadow Ridge Golf Club](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/96508.jpg)

*Photo: Marriott's Shadow Ridge Golf Club via GolfNow*

**18 holes, par 72 · Resort, open to the public** · [golfshadowridge.com](https://www.golfshadowridge.com/)

A Nick Faldo design with a links-inspired feel, framed by the Santa Rosa Mountains. Expect generous fairways, deep bunkering and firm, fast greens. WhereGolf rates it among the valley's must-play courses.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Dolce](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-desert/dolce): newer single-family homes, many built 2022–2023, a short distance from the course.

### Mountain Vista Golf Club at Sun City Palm Desert: San Gorgonio & Santa Rosa

![A hole on the Santa Rosa Course at Mountain Vista Golf Club](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/129888.jpg)

*Photo: Mountain Vista Golf Club via GolfNow*

**36 holes (two 18s), par 72 · Pay-as-you-play, open to the public** · [mountainvistagolfclub.com](https://www.mountainvistagolfclub.com/)

Two Billy Casper designs inside Sun City Palm Desert, open to anyone. Reviewers give both courses high marks for conditions, pace and value, and the mountain views live up to the name.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Sun City Palm Desert](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-desert/sun-city): the valley's largest 55+ community, with about 4,950 single-level homes and three clubhouses.

### Palm Desert Resort Country Club

![The 6th hole at Palm Desert Resort Country Club](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/112012.jpg)

*Photo: Palm Desert Resort Country Club via GolfNow*

**18 holes, par 72 · Public** · [theresorter.com](https://www.theresorter.com/golf/)

A championship layout set against the Santa Rosa and San Jacinto mountains, with multiple tee options that make it playable for every level. It's a local favorite for affordable, no-fuss rounds.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Palm Desert Resort Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-desert/palm-desert-resort-country-club): condominiums from the 1980s along the fairways.

### Palm Desert Country Club

![A hole at Palm Desert Country Club](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/91641.jpg)

*Photo: Palm Desert Country Club via GolfNow*

**18 holes · Public** · [Palm Desert Country Club](https://underpar.com/courses/palm-desert-country-club)

For about 50 years this course has been a go-to for public golf in Palm Desert, and it has been nominated for Best Public Golf Course in the Coachella Valley by Coachella Valley Weekly. It pairs a storied history with a modernized layout and a friendly neighborhood vibe.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Palm Desert Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-desert/palm-desert-country-club): one of the city's original neighborhoods, with mostly 1960s single-family homes around the course and no gates.

### Shadow Mountain Golf Club

![Aerial view of Shadow Mountain Golf Club](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/120494.jpg)

*Photo: Shadow Mountain Golf Club via GolfNow*

**18 holes, par 71 · Public** · [golfshadowmountain.com](https://www.golfshadowmountain.com/)

One of Palm Desert's historic courses, tucked into a residential neighborhood just south of El Paseo. It's a classic layout that rewards accuracy over power, and it sits just minutes from El Paseo's shopping and dining.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [El Paseo Village](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-desert/el-paseo-village): condominiums near the course and steps from El Paseo shopping and dining.

### Desert Falls Country Club

*Photo: Desert Falls Country Club via GolfNow*

**18 holes, par 72 · Semi-private, open to the public** · [desertfallsgolf.com](https://www.desertfallsgolf.com/)

A Ron Fream design and a longtime favorite among semi-private clubs, with mountain views and an easy-to-reach central Palm Desert location.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Desert Falls Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-desert/desert-falls-country-club): condominiums built around 1989 along the course.

### Woodhaven Country Club

![Fairway at Woodhaven Country Club](https://www.woodhavencc.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/9918/2026/05/banner-new-1.jpg?w=1920\&h=500\&crop=1)

*Photo: Woodhaven Country Club*

**18 holes, par 70 · Semi-private, open to the public** · [woodhavencc.com](https://www.woodhavencc.com/golf)

A shorter par-70 layout at a relaxed, friendly club where public golfers feel welcome. It's a great pick for a quick round.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Woodhaven Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-desert/woodhaven-country-club): condominiums from the mid-1980s set along the fairways.

## Rancho Mirage

### Westin Rancho Mirage: Pete Dye Resort Course

![The Pete Dye Resort Course at the Westin Rancho Mirage](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/ttf/image/hdm/9620.jpg)

*Photo: The Westin Rancho Mirage Golf Resort & Spa via GolfNow*

**18 holes, par 70 · Resort, open to the public** · [Westin Rancho Mirage golf](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/pspwi-the-westin-rancho-mirage-golf-resort-and-spa/golf/)

Pete Dye built this course in 1987, and it carries all his trademarks: railroad ties, pot bunkers, elevated tees and tucked pins. It reopened on August 29, 2026, after a project that installed brand-new championship greens. Fans call it one of Dye's most enjoyable resort layouts.

**Nearby neighborhoods:** [Mission Hills Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/rancho-mirage/mission-hills-country-club), with single-family homes and condos on one of the valley's best-known golf properties, and [Del Webb Rancho Mirage](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/rancho-mirage/del-webb-rm), a 55+ community built from 2018.

### Mission Hills North: Gary Player Signature Course

![Aerial view of the 9th and 18th holes at Mission Hills North](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/121779.jpg)

*Photo: Mission Hills North via GolfNow*

**18 holes, par 72 · Daily fee/resort** · [jcgolf.com/mission-hills-north-golf-course](https://www.jcgolf.com/mission-hills-north-golf-course)

The first and only Gary Player design in the desert, opened in the early 1990s. Water comes into play on about a dozen holes, the greens are small and shapely, and the San Jacinto Mountains frame the back nine. GolfPass reviewers rate the pace of play highly, and it's long been a favorite of both locals and resort guests.

**Nearby neighborhoods:** [Mira Vista](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/rancho-mirage/mira-vista), a gated community of single-family homes built 2001–2003 next to the course, and [Del Webb Rancho Mirage](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/rancho-mirage/del-webb-rm) just to the east.

### Desert Island Country Club

![Golfers on the green at Desert Island Country Club](https://di71.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/sarah-barlow-4022_home_verdantgolf-1-800x800.webp)

*Photo: Desert Island Country Club*

**18 holes, par 72 · Semi-private, open to the public** · [di71.com](https://di71.com/)

A one-of-a-kind setting: the residences sit on an island in the middle of a man-made lake, with the course wrapped around the outside, so most holes play without homes alongside. Golfers enjoy the open, uncluttered feel and the classic Rancho Mirage location on Frank Sinatra Drive.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Desert Island](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/rancho-mirage/desert-island): condominiums on the island, mostly from the 1970s, with lake and course views.

### Omni Rancho Las Palmas Golf

![A sunny day at Omni Rancho Las Palmas golf course](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/90669.jpg)

*Photo: Omni Rancho Las Palmas Resort via GolfNow*

**27 holes (North, South & West nines) · Resort, open to the public** · [Omni Rancho Las Palmas golf](https://www.omnihotels.com/hotels/palm-springs-rancho-las-palmas/golf)

Three nines winding through a lush resort setting. The shorter, friendly layouts are a favorite with resort guests, couples and newer golfers, and the 27-hole setup keeps tee times flexible.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Rancho Las Palmas Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/rancho-mirage/rancho-las-palmas-country-club): condominiums built around 1977 along the resort's fairways.

## Cathedral City

### Cimarron Golf Resort: Boulder & Pebble

![The Boulder Course at Cimarron Golf Resort](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/130765.jpg)

*Photo: Cimarron Golf Resort via GolfNow*

**Boulder: 18 holes, par 71 · Pebble: 18-hole short course · Public** · [cimarrongolf.com](https://cimarrongolf.com/)

Two courses side by side. The Boulder is a full-length layout with mountain views, and the Pebble is a short course that's ideal for beginners, juniors and anyone sharpening their irons. A popular, relaxed choice for groups.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Desert Princess](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/cathedral-city/desert-princess), just up Landau Boulevard (see below).

### Desert Princess Country Club

![View over the water at Desert Princess Country Club](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/32373.jpg)

*Photo: Desert Princess Country Club via GolfNow*

**27 holes · Semi-private, open to the public** · [desertprincesscc.com](https://www.desertprincesscc.com/)

Three nines that can be played in different combinations, with lakes and mountain views throughout. Golfers like the variety and the easygoing country-club feel.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Desert Princess](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/cathedral-city/desert-princess): a mix of single-family homes and condos on the course, built from the late 1980s onward.

### Cathedral Canyon Golf Club

![The 3rd hole at Cathedral Canyon Golf Club](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/105134.jpg)

*Photo: Cathedral Canyon Golf Club via GolfNow*

**18 holes, par 70 · Public** · [jcgolf.com](https://www.jcgolf.com/san-diego-tee-times/golf-courses-in-palm-springs-ca)

Managed by JC Golf, Cathedral Canyon is a longtime Cathedral City favorite. Its shorter par-70 layout and water features make it a fun, approachable round for mid- and high-handicappers.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Cathedral Canyon Country Club](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/cathedral-city/cathedral-canyon-country-club): mostly condos from the late 1980s set along the course.

## Palm Springs

### Indian Canyons Golf Resort: North & South

![View from the 17th tee on the South Course at Indian Canyons Golf Resort](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/91597.jpg)

*Photo: Indian Canyons Golf Resort via GolfNow*

**36 holes (two 18s), par 72 · Resort/public** · [indiancanyonsgolf.com](https://www.indiancanyonsgolf.com/)

Classic Palm Springs golf at the foot of the San Jacinto Mountains, owned by the Agua Caliente Band of Cahuilla Indians. The North Course was once a private club, and its clubhouse still displays photos of Sinatra and friends. The South Course is the more scenic of the two, tucked right against the mountains. Reviewers consistently mention the views and well-kept fairways, and during summer one 18 typically stays open while the other is refreshed.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Indian Canyons](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-springs/indian-canyons): 1970s-era single-family homes along the fairways, many with direct mountain views.

### Tahquitz Creek Golf Resort: Legend & Resort

![Aerial view of the Legend Course at Tahquitz Creek Golf Resort](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/102168.jpg)

*Photo: Tahquitz Creek Golf Resort via GolfNow*

**36 holes (two 18s), par 72 · City-owned, public** · [tahquitzgolfresort.com](https://www.tahquitzgolfresort.com/golf/)

Palm Springs' own municipal golf complex. The Legend Course is the classic, tree-lined original, and the newer Resort Course offers a more open, modern feel. Locals appreciate the central location, the value and having two very different layouts at one address.

**Nearby neighborhoods:** [Los Compadres](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-springs/los-compadres), with late-1970s single-family homes beside the course, and the established Demuth Park area just north.

### Escena Golf Club

![Lake and fairways at Escena Golf Club in Palm Springs](https://escenagolf.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/EGC-15.jpg)

*Photo: Escena Golf Club*

**18 holes, par 72 · Public** · [escenagolf.com](https://escenagolf.com/)

A Jack Nicklaus design on the north side of Palm Springs, near the airport. Many golfers say it has some of the best mountain views in the valley, with wide fairways and a modern clubhouse and grill that make it a favorite for an easy, scenic round.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Escena](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/palm-springs/escena): newer single-family homes, mostly built from 2014 to 2020, set along the course.

## Desert Hot Springs

### Mission Lakes Country Club

![The 2nd green over the water at Mission Lakes Country Club](https://exddilid.cdn.imgeng.in/app/courses/image/preview/9280.jpg)

*Photo: Mission Lakes Country Club via GolfNow*

**18 holes, par 71 · Semi-private, open to the public** · Golf shop: (760) 329-8061

A Ted Robinson design from 1972, set in the foothills north of I-10. Golfers like the elevated tees, the sweeping views back across the valley and the cooler, breezier conditions up the hill. It's one of the friendliest-priced 18-hole rounds in the north valley.

**Nearby neighborhood:** [Mission Lakes](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/desert-hot-springs/mission-lakes): single-family homes and condos built mostly from the 1980s to 2000s, wrapped around the course.

## Short courses, par-3s and executive layouts

Not every great round needs four and a half hours. These courses are ideal for beginners, juniors, a quick nine after work or sharpening your short game:

- **The Lights at Indio** (Indio): 18-hole par 54, lighted for night play. See above.

- **Shadow Hills North** (Indio): 18-hole par 54, nationally ranked. See above.

- **Palm Royale Country Club** (La Quinta): an 18-hole, par-54 Ted Robinson design from 1986, open to the public. [palmroyalecc.com](https://www.palmroyalecc.com/) · Nearby: [Palm Royale](https://jpsrealtor.com/neighborhoods/la-quinta/palm-royale) condominiums around the course.

- **The Golf Center at Palm Desert** (Palm Desert): a 9-hole par-3 and practice facility, home of The First Tee of the Coachella Valley. [golfcenterpd.com](https://www.golfcenterpd.com/)

- **Oasis Country Club** (Palm Desert): an 18-hole, par-60 executive course that rewards controlled play. [theoasiscountryclub.com](https://www.theoasiscountryclub.com/)

- **Suncrest Country Club** (Palm Desert): a 9-hole, par-33 layout in the foothills with valley views. [suncrestcountryclub.net](https://www.suncrestcountryclub.net/)

- **Cimarron Pebble Course** (Cathedral City): 18-hole short course next to the Boulder. [cimarrongolf.com](https://cimarrongolf.com/golf)

- **Date Palm Country Club** (Cathedral City): an 18-hole, par-58 executive course. Call ahead for public tee times. [datepalmcountryclub.net](https://www.datepalmcountryclub.net/)

- **The Sands RV & Golf Resort** (Desert Hot Springs): a casual short course with nine-hole rates.

## Courses you may still see listed online

A few courses still appear on older lists and maps but are closed:

- **Desert Dunes Golf Club** (Desert Hot Springs) closed in 2025; the land is planned to become a nature preserve.

- **Mesquite Golf & Country Club** (Palm Springs) closed in 2022 and was donated to the Oswit Land Trust.

- **Rancho Mirage Country Club** and **Palm Springs Country Club** are long closed.
